## Content and quality
- Start with a concrete independent traveller's question, not a promotional headline. Examples: Almaty airport to town; 4-night Almaty itinerary; Kolsai/Kaindy transport; travel costs; winter packing; Bishkek to Issyk-Kul; Karakol base selection; Kazakhstan/Kyrgyzstan combined itinerary.
- Search current Korean results and official/operator sources before writing. Search demand is a hypothesis unless analytics supports it; don't invent search-volume numbers. Use at least two relevant sources when making factual travel claims. Check transport, prices, hours, seasonal access and entry requirements freshly. Resolve contradictory sources using the responsible authority; omit unverified precise claims.
- Answer the query early. Give usable decisions, tradeoffs, route steps, budget inclusions or a checklist as appropriate. No fixed word quota, filler, repetitive templates, keyword stuffing, fabricated first-person travel experience, invented staff expertise/reviews or guarantees. Write natural Korean. Attribute as 트립소다 카자흐스탄 콘텐츠팀. No pretending a human personally inspected AI-researched text.
- Include checked date and supporting source links. Respect copyright; summarize rather than copy. Images remain optional: publish a useful, verified guide without an image rather than spending credits or using uncertain media.
- Prefer an existing owned or licensed photograph when its publishing rights and credit are confirmed. Use `kind: "photograph"`, record `credit`, `rights`, and an HTTPS `sourceUrl` when applicable. Never reuse an image whose rights are unclear.
- Generate a Higgsfield image only when it materially improves the guide and the connected workflow has an explicitly approved per-run credit budget. Preserve credits for the marketing team; do not generate an image merely to satisfy a daily quota, and do not silently substitute another generator.
- Ask Higgsfield for a natural, photorealistic travel-image tone: restrained saturation, plausible light and weather, realistic scale and anatomy, and no invented readable signs or documentary claims. Avoid exaggerated scenery and awkward details. Generated imagery illustrates the topic; it is not evidence that the depicted moment happened.
- Store an authorized image at `public/images/articles/<slug>-hero.<ext>` and add optional `heroImage` metadata with pixel `width` and `height`, descriptive alt text, an accurate caption, and recorded publishing rights. For generated output use `kind: "ai-generated"` and `provider: "Higgsfield"`; keep generation provenance in internal metadata; do not show provider names, account details, publishing approvals or generation-process descriptions to readers (user instruction, 2026-10-28). Never call generated output a real, on-location, or documentary photograph. A rights-confirmed photograph uses `kind: "photograph"` and displays its photo credit. Publishing-rights notes are internal metadata, not public captions. Captions should explain the travel topic naturally without implying that illustrative imagery documents a real place or service.
- Subtle conversion: explain independent options first. Where vehicles, guides or lodging are useful, naturally offer help for just that segment. End with a short contextual invitation to /#proposal. Don't promise unverified Kyrgyzstan operations or package prices. No medical-tour content in this Korean travel publication.
- Compare all existing titles, keywords and article bodies; avoid cannibalizing an existing query. Pick a genuinely different question. No future dates or republishing the same article to meet quotas.
